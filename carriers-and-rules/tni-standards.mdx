A **TNI standard** is TNI's own fixed answer for a question the intake doesn't answer. It's the one kind of answer the extension may give without intake support, and only when it's recorded as TNI's. Standards are filled, marked **blue** on the page, tagged **TNI standard** in the panel, and become check items in [review mode](/carriers-and-rules/rules#review-mode).

<Note>
An intake answer always wins over a standard. Most standards also step aside, leaving the question to the agent with the line quoted, when the intake's notes point the other way.
</Note>

## Never defaulted

These are client facts or attestations. When the intake doesn't answer them, they're the agent's, and the AI never sees them:

- Loss runs and tenure with the current carrier, except where a standard below says otherwise
- Continuous coverage when the intake doesn't show current insurance
- Other business coverages when the notes say the client has general liability
- A USDOT record whose registered name differs from the intake's business
- The NJ vehicle registrant, a missing ownership answer, marital status
- The owner's date of birth when no driver matches the owner
- On GEICO: anything the intake doesn't answer and no GEICO standard covers

## How a standard gets added

Standards come from TNI, usually after a live run where the agent answered the same question by hand. Each one is recorded with its date in [`docs/intake-and-rules.md`](https://github.com/lutfikeskin/tni-rater-autofill/blob/main/docs/intake-and-rules.md), implemented as a rule with `source: 'standard'`, and, for Progressive and AmWINS, stated in the AI rulebook ([`src/ai/tni-rules.js`](https://github.com/lutfikeskin/tni-rater-autofill/blob/main/src/ai/tni-rules.js)) so Luna can cite it.

Repeated agent answers and AI acceptance aren't enough to make a standard. Logs can suggest one; only TNI decides.
