Each carrier is a module in [`src/carriers/`](https://github.com/lutfikeskin/tni-rater-autofill/tree/main/src/carriers), registered in [`carriers.js`](https://github.com/lutfikeskin/tni-rater-autofill/blob/main/src/carriers.js). Its config (`CarrierConfig` in `types.js`) holds its hosts, rules, helper actions, Continue pattern, steps and stop points.

## Side by side

| | Progressive | AmWINS | GEICO |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pages | Duck Creek, one address per page | One address, five steps | Single-page app, one address, nine steps |
| Finds questions by | `fieldref` data-model name, else text | Selectors and labels | `Id_<Name>_<n>` name stem, by step and form |
| JavaScript world | `MAIN` (ExtJS) | Isolated | Isolated, through shadow roots |
| Generic label rules | Off | On, kept out of row sections | Off |
| Answers per round | One (server checks each) | Whole round (`batch: true`) | One |
| Page or step read from | The URL's `pageName` | Visible headings | Progress bar, confirmed by the page title |
| Continue | Continue / "Ok, start quote." | Save & Continue | Next, Save and Continue, Looks Good |
| Live status | Mapped from live MD, OH, NC quotes; many live runs | Live runs since October 2026 | Mapped from saved pages and captures; first live runs 2026-10-21 |

## Progressive

<div className="pele-diagram">
```text
flowchart TB
  subgraph APP["The application"]
    direction LR
    B["Business"] --> V["Vehicles<br/>and trailers"] --> D["Drivers"] --> M["More about<br/>business"] --> F["Filings"]
  end
  subgraph RATE["Rating and the quote"]
    direction LR
    R["Rates<br/>CoveragesRates"] --> P["Print<br/>agent clicks Print/Send"]
  end
  APP --> RATE
  B -. "existing policy" .-> X["DeclinationNotice<br/>stop page"]
  B -. "owner not verified" .-> N["NoHit<br/>agent's page"]
```
</div>

- **Helpers, each clicked once and in order:** Verify DOT, Add a Vehicle, the vehicle tile, Lookup VIN, Add Trailer, the trailer tile, Add Driver, cargo **+** → Add a Commodity → Done with this coverage, general liability **+** → Done (when the intake asks for GL), the bill plan, Recalculate, then Print.
- **Never continued:** NoHit (the agent adjusts the owner's name), the Print page (Chrome opens the quote PDF only after the agent's own Print/Send click), and the finish and buy pages.
- **Stop page:** DeclinationNotice, when the client already has a Progressive commercial auto policy. Nothing is filled or clicked.
- **One answer at a time**, because Progressive checks every answer with its server; new follow-up questions are picked up as they appear.
- **Renumbering:** Progressive renumbered every question id on 2026-10-07. Rules hold the id they were written for only as a name.

## AmWINS

<div className="pele-diagram">
```text
flowchart TB
  subgraph APP["Five steps on one address, each ending in Save & Continue"]
    direction LR
    I["Insured Info"] --> D["Drivers"] --> V["Vehicles<br/>VIN import"] --> C["Coverages"] --> U["Underwriting<br/>trailers as text"]
  end
  APP --> DEC(["Declaration:<br/>the agent's attestation"])
```
</div>

- **Helpers:** the Auto tile and Open New Application to start, Add Driver for each extra driver, **Import Data** and **Import Valid Records** for the VIN list (verified by the rows changing), and **Default all answers to No** on Underwriting (skipped while an agent's answer is on the step).
- **Vehicle rows** are found by their VIN (`witness`), since Import Valid Records draws them in its own order.
- **No trailer section:** each intake trailer becomes a line of the Underwriting disclosure text, such as `Trailer - 2015 Great Dane Dry Van - VIN … - $25K`.
- **Never submitted:** the declaration is the agent's, so Save & Continue never gets past it.

## GEICO

<div className="pele-diagram">
```text
flowchart TB
  GW["Agents' gateway<br/>Start New Quote ZIP, USDOT, product"]
  subgraph APP["The application: one address, step read from the progress bar"]
    direction LR
    S1["Business Class<br/>& USDOT"] --> S2["Business &<br/>Owner Info"] --> S3["Vehicles<br/>one form per unit"] --> S4["Drivers &<br/>Incidents"]
  end
  subgraph QUOTE["The quote"]
    direction LR
    S5["Additional<br/>Business Info"] --> S6["Quote & Coverages<br/>Recalculate, pay plan,<br/>Print Quote Proposal"]
  end
  subgraph STOP["The agent's steps: nothing filled or clicked"]
    direction LR
    S7["Final Quote<br/>Details"] --> S8["MVR & CLUE<br/>paid reports"] --> S9["Payment<br/>Information"]
  end
  GW --> APP --> QUOTE --> STOP
  classDef stop stroke-dasharray: 5 4
  class S7,S8,S9 stop
```
</div>

- **One address for every step.** The step is read from GEICO's progress bar, and only while the page title names the same step. When they disagree mid-redraw, the fill reads nothing and asks to fill again.
- **Units one form at a time.** Vehicles and Drivers & Incidents show one unit's form at a time. Rules name the form by a question only it asks (`whenShown`), and a form filled for another unit is held until the agent picks its unit.
- **Adding units:** with **Fill each page as soon as it opens** or **Click Continue when the page is done** on, the fill adds each intake vehicle, trailer and driver not on the quote yet (the list's **+**, Vehicle or Trailer on the chooser, Next), once per unit.
- **Quote & Coverages:** opens the cargo card when the intake asks for cargo, then once nothing is left and the page has been quiet for 2 seconds, clicks **Recalculate** once, waits for the premium, rereads every answer, picks the pay plan and clicks **Print Quote Proposal**. Answers GEICO changed back are put back (the fill's) or left (the agent's) and marked red.
- **Stop steps** (dashed): Final Quote Details, MVR & CLUE and Payment Information are the agent's. MVR and CLUE reports cost the agency, so TNI stops a GEICO quote at Quote & Coverages and prints the proposal there.
- **Never filled:** the owner's Social Security number.

<Note>
GEICO's support is mapped from saved Nebraska pages and Florida captures, with its first live runs on 2026-10-21. The mock (`npm run mock:geico`) checks behavior against GEICO-like pages, not GEICO's own scripts.
</Note>

## The local test carrier

`carriers.js` also registers a `test` carrier for `localhost`, and maps the practice copies served from the repository (`test/replay/`, `test/fixtures/amwins/`, `test/fixtures/geico/`) to the real carriers' rules. Practice pages fill like the real ones but aren't recorded as quotes.
